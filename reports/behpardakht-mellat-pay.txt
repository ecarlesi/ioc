## Description
Two-stage Persian-language kit: an adult-services landing page funnel feeds
into a near-pixel-perfect clone of the Behpardakht Mellat (به پرداخت ملت)
Iranian bank payment gateway. Harvests full card data (PAN, PIN2, CVV2,
expiry, OTP/captcha), Luhn-validates and BIN-maps it against 27 Iranian
banks, relays to an external C2 panel, then exfiltrates to Telegram.
Multi-card harvesting via a fake "payment failed, try another card" page.
